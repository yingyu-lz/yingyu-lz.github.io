About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Forschungszentrum Jülich, Germany. My research focuses on atmospheric environmental chemistry, particularly the oxidation of gas-phase organic compounds and the formation mechanisms of secondary organic aerosol (SOA).

Research Interests

  • Atmospheric oxidation chemistry
  • Nighttime oxidation of volatile organic compounds
  • Secondary organic aerosol formation
  • Atmospheric observations and mass spectrometry

Education and Experience

About Me个人简介

I am a Postdoc at Forschungszentrum Jülich, Germany, supervised by Soeren Zorn. Before that, I received my Ph.D. and B.Sc. from Peking University.

My reserarch focuses on secondary organic aerosol (SOA) formation from biogenic and anthropogenic volatile organic compounds (VOCs), as well as their mixtures.

我目前在 德国于利希研究中心从事博士后研究，导师为 Soeren, Zorn。此前在北京大学获得博士和学士学位。

我的研究方向主要包括 人为源、生物源及其混合体系二次有机气溶胶生成机制。

  • Atmospheric Chemistry大气化学
  • Secondary Organic Aerosols二次有机气溶胶
  • Chamber Simulation烟雾箱模拟
  • High-resolution Mass Spectrometry高分辨率质谱
